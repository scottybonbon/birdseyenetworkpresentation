    11 · The path

    Four phases. Starts humble. Ends big.

    Phase 0 · Align & proveJul–Aug 2026

    Win the room. Prove the raw material.

    Town-hall vision + mandate, measure detection at 3–5 sites, clear the legal path. Nothing scales until the team believes it and counsel signs off.

    Phase 1 · Concierge pilotSep–Oct 2026

    Ship the digest. Send alerts by hand.

    2–3 sites. Digest first, opt-in carrier alerts, courtesy calls, first permissioned contacts. Deliberately small.

    Phase 2 · OperationalizeOct 2026–Feb 2027

    Automate the machine. Turn contacts into pipeline.

    25–50 sites. Automated with human review, BDR mapping on the white space, first warm-sourced closes.

    Phase 3 · Growth engineMar–Jun 2027

    The big bang: the network as the business.

    Full footprint as the acquisition engine, plus the productized horizon: carrier portal, paid tiers, the yard's system of record.
